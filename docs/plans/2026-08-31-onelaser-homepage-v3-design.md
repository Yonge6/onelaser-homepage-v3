# OneLaser Homepage V3 Design

## Goal

Create an independent OneLaser homepage that moves visitors through one clear decision story: featured campaigns, machine fit, finished outcomes, performance reasons, audience fit, real-world proof, ownership support, and next actions.

## Direction

V3 uses a restrained product-editorial system rather than a sequence of equal-weight card grids. Certia, white and `#F5F5F7` surfaces, 24–32 px radii, generous spacing, and limited OneLaser red remain the visual foundation. Large stages carry the main message; compact rails and bordered editorial rows carry supporting information.

## Page architecture

1. Hero Campaigns keeps the accessible three-story campaign carousel and direct linked actions.
2. Meet the OneLaser Lineup explains the job fit for Cobra, XRF, Hydra, and VertiGo before specifications.
3. Made with OneLaser presents a reduced, material-filtered rail of finished work and preserves the existing accessible project detail modal.
4. Why OneLaser Performs Better concentrates RF, controlled motion, vision workflow, structure, and U.S. engineering into three large alternating stories plus a compact proof rail.
5. Built for Every Ambition replaces three simultaneous cards with one tab-controlled stage for Makers, Business, and Education.
6. OneLaser in the Real World keeps static video covers and loads YouTube only after interaction.
7. The OneLaser Standard places company, training, policy, and technical-support proof close to the decision point.
8. Explore and Final CTA connect official blog, community, demo, machine collection, consultation, and subscription paths.

## Content and safety

- VertiGo appears only for cylindrical goods.
- Mixed and non-cylindrical work recommends XRF, Cobra, or Hydra.
- XRF motion copy uses `1,200 mm/s` and `True 3.5G`.
- No invented discounts, reviews, urgency, shipping dates, income promises, or checkout paths.
- Any project economics remain labeled illustrative inside the existing modal.
- Product, Support, Community, and policy names link only to official `1laser.com` destinations.

## Interaction and verification

Hero, material filters, project modal, Mega Menu, ambition tabs, video rail/modal, mobile navigation, and back-to-top control remain keyboard accessible. Images retain the seven-palette placeholder and real-pixel ready/error states. Acceptance requires `npm run verify`, desktop and 390 px browser checks, no horizontal overflow, loaded natural image dimensions, no console errors, independent V3 repository deployment, and cache-busted live DOM/order/link validation.
